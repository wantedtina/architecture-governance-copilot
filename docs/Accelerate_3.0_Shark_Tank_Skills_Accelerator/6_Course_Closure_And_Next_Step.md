# Learn from the Best

In addition to the internal examples, take time to watch real-world
Shark Tank pitches on Shark Tank Global - YouTube. As you watch, pay
attention to:

-   How entrepreneurs open their pitch.
-   The way they articulate customer pain points.
-   Techniques used to explain value and impact.
-   Storytelling approaches that create engagement.
-   Confidence, presence, and delivery style under questioning.

# Your Final Challenge

As you prepare for pitch day, ask yourself:

-   Can I clearly explain the opportunity in under one minute?
-   Is my business value obvious and measurable?
-   Does my pitch tell a compelling story?
-   Am I prepared to answer difficult questions?
-   Would I invest in my own idea after hearing this pitch?

If you can confidently answer "Yes" to these questions, you are ready to
take the stage.

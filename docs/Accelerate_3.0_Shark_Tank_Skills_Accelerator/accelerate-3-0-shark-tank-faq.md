# Accelerate 3.0 Shark Tank FAQ

## Shark Tank

### What is a Shark Tank?

The name Shark Tank comes from an American TV show where businesses
pitch to investors --- or sharks --- for investment in their ideas. It's
also known as Dragons' Den in other countries.

Unlike an internal project, each project is invested --- so all you have
to do is convince the Sharks your solution is investable.

### What will the pitch to the Sharks look like?

Each team will have **10 minutes** to introduce and demonstrate their
idea to the sharks and there will be **5 minutes of Q&A**.

Timing will be very strict to ensure that all the finalists get to
present.

**Hint:** Watch the actual Shark Tank show to practice your pitch and
stand out!

### What will the Sharks/judges be looking out for?

The sharks will be looking for working solutions or ideas that can
accelerate our path to being a Client Focused, Data Driven Digital bank
from their particular point of view.

### Can we get help preparing for our pitch?

Yes, you will get coaching sessions to help you prepare your final
pitch.

### What are the next steps for topics that are the Shark Tank winners?

Winning ideas still follow the normal SDLC and production process. There
is no fast-track bypass. However, the initiative receives priority
attention, potential funding and support to help take it forward.

# Shared video visual direction

The accepted V1/V2 artifacts and current user instructions take precedence over generic tool styling defaults.

Use navy/white backgrounds, restrained blue/green accents and readable sans-serif typography. Preserve the accepted title and closing artwork, logo proportions, team credits and real application styling. Keep English captions legible, at most two lines where practical, with clear separation from controls. Retain visible cursor/click guidance without obscuring evidence.

Keep the continuous process flow and equal visual standing of Project Team and Domain Architect. The architecture describes the agreed system design without Production/Target/fake title labels. Do not restore the earlier requirement to mark every unimplemented component as a dashed Future branch; that convention was superseded by the user's architecture direction.

The original detailed palette, source links and historical observations are preserved unchanged at `archive/pre-v1/BRAND_AND_VISUAL_GUIDE.md`. They record the earlier review, not a new brand/legal or live-source verification. Shared accepted artwork is in `assets/opening-title.png` and `assets/closing-card.png`.
